Teaching resources

Interactive tools

Tools I build for my own classroom, mostly for the first-year seminar Our Universe: 95% Unknown. Each one runs entirely in your browser: nothing to install, no account, and they keep working offline once the page has loaded. Free for anyone to use or adapt for teaching.

Cosmology

Expansion Explorer

Set the expansion rate and how much matter and vacuum energy a universe holds, and watch how that decides its whole history — whether it had a Big Bang, whether the expansion speeds up or slows down, and whether it expands forever or falls back. A pie chart on the plot shows what the universe is made of at any moment you pick.

 Data analysis

Hubble Fitter

Fit real data and see where the Hubble constant actually comes from. Hubble's own 1929 galaxies give H₀ ≈ 423, six times the modern value; the 1998 supernovae that revealed the accelerating universe give 63. You choose the model — a line through the origin or one with an intercept, the supernova slope fixed at 5 or left free — and the fit itself is least squares. Seeing how far off Hubble was, and why, does more than being told the number.
